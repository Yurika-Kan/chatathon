Why are trial users abandoning setup?

Campco synthesis from customer language, competitor creative, and product behavior.

18 sources reviewed

3 angles worth testing

Hypothesis 01

The empty-canvas problem

High confidence

Teams are not resisting automation. They are leaving when setup feels like another blank brief to fill out before Campco demonstrates value.

“I wanted a useful first draft, but the tool asked me to explain our whole company again.”

Trial exit interview, operations lead

Campaign angle: Show the first campaign appearing from existing company media, before asking for refinements.

Hypothesis 02

Proof beats feature volume

Medium confidence

Buyers engage longer when campaign examples lead with a measurable outcome. In the reviewed ads, 2.2x longer watch time came from outcome-led creative rather than feature tours.

“Do not tell me it makes content faster. Show me the week of work it replaced.”

Reddit discussion, growth marketing community

Campaign angle: Lead with a before-and-after campaign timeline, then reveal the workflow behind it.

Emerging signal

Control is part of the promise

Needs evidence

Several teams want AI speed, but still expect a visible approval point before assets leave the workspace.
